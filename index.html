Welcome to Campus Lost and Found!

lost something on campus? found something that belongs to someone else? this website helps students share information and reconnect people with their belongings.

How it works

  1. Check the lost items and found items pages.
  2. If you cannot find what you need, submit a report.
  3. Use the contact information in a listing to arrange a safe handover.
